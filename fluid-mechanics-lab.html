Virtual Fluid Mechanics Lab

the apparatus

The pipe-flow rig in our fluids lab is only available during lab hours, and if you fall behind or just want more practice, there's no way back in. So I rebuilt it — the apparatus, the maths, and the marking — as something that lives in a browser instead of a timetable slot.

How it's put together

It's a full-stack React + Vite + TailwindCSS app with an Express backend. The apparatus itself is an animated SVG that responds to live parameter input, so turning a dial actually changes what you see rather than just updating a number on the side.

The part I spent the most time on wasn't the physics — it was making the validation module forgiving enough that a slightly-off calculation still tells you where you went wrong, instead of just marking it red.

What it actually does

Run the apparatus, get your working checked against the major-loss equations, test yourself with a quiz/viva module, and download a generated PDF report at the end. It's meant to feel like a lab station you could sit at unsupervised and still leave with something valid.
