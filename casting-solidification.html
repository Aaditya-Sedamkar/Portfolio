ANSYS Fluent, casting

AL6061-T6 Sand Casting Solidification

the mesh

Most casting defects — porosity, shrinkage, hot spots — trace back to how the solidification front moves through the mould as the metal cools. It's a lot cheaper to simulate that moment than to figure out afterward why a real casting came out wrong.

Running the simulation

I modelled and meshed the casting geometry and sand mould for a transient thermal simulation in ANSYS Fluent, tracking the solidification front as AL6061-T6 cools inside it.

Watched that front crawl across the mesh for longer than I'd like to admit. Worth the stare — most of what goes wrong in a casting happens right there.

Writing it up

The findings were compiled into a formal synopsis, formatted to RVCE's Design Thinking Lab template — the kind of document meant to hold up next to the simulation itself, not just summarise it.
