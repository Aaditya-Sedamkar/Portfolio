Simulation — ANSYS Fluent

AL6061-T6 Sand Casting Solidification

A thermal simulation of the solidification front as AL6061-T6 cools in a sand mould, run in ANSYS Fluent for the Design Thinking Lab.

ToolANSYS Fluent
MaterialAL6061-T6
ProcessSand casting
FocusSolidification front

Method

The casting geometry and sand mould were modelled and meshed for a transient thermal simulation in ANSYS Fluent, tracking how the solidification front advances through the AL6061-T6 casting as it cools.

Why solidification matters

Most casting defects — porosity, shrinkage, hot spots — trace back to how the solidification front moves through the mould. Simulating that front before a real pour is cheaper than diagnosing a defective casting after one.

Findings were compiled into a synopsis document, formatted to RVCE's Design Thinking Lab report template.
