HVAC Sustainability Case Study

A case study examining sustainability trade-offs in HVAC system design, developed for the Design Thinking Lab.

CourseDesign Thinking Lab (DTL)
FocusHVAC systems
LensSustainability trade-offs
OutputCase study report

Approach

The case study was developed following the Design Thinking Lab's structure — framing the problem, mapping the relevant HVAC design choices, and evaluating each against sustainability criteria before drawing conclusions.

Framing the case

HVAC systems sit at the intersection of occupant comfort, energy consumption and equipment lifecycle — optimising for one often costs another. This case study works through those trade-offs for a real system rather than treating sustainability as a single checkbox.

Compiled into a case study report formatted to RVCE's official DTL template.
