HVAC Sustainability Case Study

the schematic

HVAC systems sit at the intersection of comfort, energy use, and equipment lifecycle — and optimising for one usually costs one of the others. This case study, for the Design Thinking Lab, works through those trade-offs for a real system instead of treating sustainability as a single checkbox.

How I approached it

Following the Design Thinking Lab's structure: framing the problem, mapping out the relevant HVAC design choices, and weighing each one against sustainability criteria before drawing any conclusions.

The trade-offs are rarely as clean as a brochure makes them look — every choice that saves energy seems to cost something else, usually comfort or upfront cost.

Where it ended up

Compiled into a case study report, formatted to RVCE's official Design Thinking Lab template.
