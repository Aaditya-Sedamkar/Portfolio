LabFab: A Custom Lab Electrode Fabricator

A prototype platform for automated electrode fabrication, consolidating surface roughening, slurry coating, drying, and calendaring into a continuous, modular workflow.

Control SystemsArduino UNO R3, L298N
ActuationNEMA-17 Steppers & DC Motors
Coating Specs50–500 µm Gap, 15 mm/min Speed
ReproducibilitySignificantly higher than manual

System Architecture & Workflow

LabFab is designed to eliminate the inconsistencies of manual electrode preparation by integrating essential processing modules into a single automated pipeline:

  • Substrate Sanding and Cleaning: Dual-stage mechanical roughening of the copper foil using a 120-grit dry sander and a 240-grit wet sander under water rinse, followed by an acetone wash to remove organic contaminants.
  • Doctor Blade Coating: A precision-adjustable (50–500 µm) doctor blade coats the substrate with a carbon-based slurry. NEMA 17 stepper motor-driven feed rollers maintain a constant linear velocity of 15 mm/min.
  • Fan-Assisted Drying: Axial DC fans provide ambient airflow across the coated surface (4.5 m/s) to enable partial solvent evaporation and stabilize the wet film prior to calendaring.
  • Calendaring: Spring-loaded 304 stainless steel rollers compress the electrode under a controlled pressure of 0.9 kN/m, densifying the coating, smoothing the surface, and reducing porosity.

Metrology & Characterization

The system's efficacy was validated through comprehensive metrology of the fabricated supercapacitor electrodes. Stylus profilometry confirmed that optimized multi-step sanding reduced surface roughness (Ra) down to 1.02 µm (400/800 grit), enhancing active material anchoring sites. Brookfield viscometry ensured the PVA-based slurry was maintained within the optimal processing range of 7.6–10.4 mPa·s.

Subsequent 12-hour soak tests in 3M Na₂SO₄ electrolyte proved that coatings produced with tighter blade tolerances and standardized substrate roughness achieved superior mechanical stability—demonstrating >90% adhesion retention without visible delamination or swelling.

Performance vs. Manual Coating

While commercial lab coaters can incur capital costs of up to ₹10 lakh, the custom LabFab system provides an open-source, modular alternative for under ₹2 lakh. More critically, it drastically reduces operator dependency. Unlike manual preparation—which often yields non-uniform thickness, streaks, and uneven edge coverage—LabFab guarantees nearly identical coatings across batches. This highly reproducible approach accelerates electrode prototyping, allowing for more reliable chronological evaluations via Cyclic Voltammetry and Chronopotentiometry.

LabFab successfully transforms the traditionally variable, multi-step manual electrode coating process into a standardized, high-throughput automated workflow perfectly suited for R&D scaling.
