EZPrint — Smart Printing Kiosk

the unit

Printing on campus usually means finding whoever has access to a printer and waiting your turn. EZPrint turns that into a kiosk problem instead — a small, self-contained unit you walk up to, submit a job on, and collect a printout from.

The build

A Raspberry Pi Zero W sits at the centre of it — small footprint, low cost, enough compute for the job. I scoped and documented the whole thing as an Experiential Learning submission, following the department's reporting structure from brief to final writeup.

This was the project that felt least like a lab exercise and most like actually designing a product someone would use — thinking about the walk-up flow mattered as much as the wiring did.

Where it landed

Delivered as a working prototype concept, with the full build documented and formatted to RVCE's Experiential Learning report standard.
