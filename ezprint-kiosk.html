Hardware — Raspberry Pi Zero W

EZPrint — Smart Printing Kiosk

A smart, self-service printing kiosk built around a Raspberry Pi Zero W — developed for the Experiential Learning course to remove the friction from getting a document printed on campus.

HardwareRaspberry Pi Zero W
CourseExperiential Learning (EL)
FocusSelf-service printing
OutputKiosk prototype + report

The build

A Raspberry Pi Zero W sits at the centre of the kiosk's hardware layout, chosen for its small footprint and low cost. The project was scoped and documented as an Experiential Learning submission, following RVCE's EL reporting structure end to end.

The brief

Printing on campus usually means finding someone with access to a printer and waiting your turn. EZPrint reframes that as a kiosk problem: a small, self-contained unit a student can walk up to, submit a job on, and collect a printout from — no intermediary required.

Delivered as a working prototype concept with full documentation formatted to RVCE's EL report standard.
