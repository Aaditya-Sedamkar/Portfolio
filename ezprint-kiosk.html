EZPrint — Smart Printing Kiosk

A smart, self-service printing kiosk built around a Raspberry Pi Zero W — developed for the Experiential Learning course to take the friction out of getting a document printed on campus.

Hardware

Raspberry Pi Zero W

Course

Experiential Learning (EL)

Focus

Self-service printing

Output

Kiosk prototype + report

The brief

Printing on campus usually means finding someone with access to a printer and waiting your turn. EZPrint reframes that as a kiosk problem: a small, self-contained unit a student can walk up to, submit a job on, and collect a printout from — no intermediary required.

Method

The Raspberry Pi Zero W was chosen as the compute core for its small footprint and low cost, sitting at the centre of the kiosk's hardware layout. The project was scoped and documented as an Experiential Learning submission, following RVCE's EL reporting structure end to end.

Result

Delivered as a working prototype concept with full documentation formatted to the department's EL report standard.
