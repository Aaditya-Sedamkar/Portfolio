Simulation — COMSOL Multiphysics

Biocorrosion of Titanium Alloys

A comparative biocorrosion simulation of three titanium alloys — Ti-6Al-4V, Ti-6Zr and Ti-8V — built to weigh their suitability for long-term implant service, where corrosion resistance matters as much as mechanical strength.

  • PhysicsTertiary Current Distribution (Nernst–Planck)
  • KineticsButler–Volmer electrode model
  • RepassivationBoundary ODE submodel
  • ResultTi-6Zr ranked strongest of the three
