About

I'm Aaditya P Sedamkar, a mechanical engineering student at RV College of Engineering, Bengaluru. My work spans simulation-heavy analysis — electrochemistry, thermal and fluid systems — alongside hands-on lab and product-style builds like embedded kiosks and virtual lab tools.

I'm also interested in entrepreneurship and product ideation alongside the core engineering work — thinking through how a technically sound idea becomes something people would actually use.

This site is a running index of that work: some of it coursework formalised into full reports, some of it self-directed builds. Placeholder frames mark where renders, screenshots and plots will go as each project's visual documentation is finalised.
